# RoutePilot for homeowners

Your menu is on the left (on a phone, the button at the top left opens it).

## Home (/dashboard)

Your account at a glance: **Your last visit**, your upcoming service, and links to **See services** and **All visits**.

## Assistant (/assistant)

This chat. It can tell you about your service, your visits and your water, and answer pool and spa questions. It can't change anything: it tells you where to do it.

## My Pools (/my-pools)

Each of your pools: what it is, its latest water test and how the water has been trending, and the **Service on this pool** (what's done, how often, and your technician). **Jump to a pool** when you have more than one.

## Service History (/history)

Every visit's report: the **Water test**, **Chemicals added**, **Parts replaced**, **What was done**, photos, and your technician's note. **More visits** loads older ones.

## Balance (/balance)

Only when your pool company bills you through RoutePilot. **What you owe for** (each visit and any other charges), **Recent payments**, and a button to pay by card. **Turn on autopay** to pay automatically each month (**Turn off autopay** to stop). Card details are only ever entered on the secure payment page, never in this chat.

## Services (/requests)

**Your services**, each with its next visit day.

- **Add a service** (or **Request a service**): pick the service and the pool, and send it. The company confirms it and the price.
- **Pause** a service while you're away: choose the **First day to skip** and the **Last day to skip**, then send the request.
- To stop a service, send a cancellation request for it.
- **Send a note** to the company about anything else.
- **Your requests** shows each request, its status and the company's reply.

The company reviews every request; nothing changes until they do.

## Your own settings

Click your name at the bottom of the menu:

- **Profile** (name, email), **Password**, **Two-factor**, **Appearance** (light or dark, **Big view** for larger text).
- **Emails** (**Email settings**): choose which emails you get: visit reports, reminders, news and offers.
