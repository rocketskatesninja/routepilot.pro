# RoutePilot for technicians

Your menu is on the left (on a phone, the button at the top left opens it). The phone icon at the top of every page (**Field app**) opens the field app, where you do your visits.

## Today (/dashboard)

Your day at a glance: your route, what's done and what's left, and anything that needs you. You can rearrange the widgets with **Customize**.

## Assistant (/assistant)

This chat. It can look up your route, your stops' details and pool history, and answer water and equipment questions. It can't change anything: it tells you where to do it. It needs signal; the field app doesn't.

## The field app

Open it with the phone icon (**Field app**) at the top of the page. It works offline: anything you finish without signal is kept on the phone and sent when you're back online (**Sync and refresh** sends it now).

- Your stops for the day are listed in order. Tap one to open the visit.
- **Water test**: enter the readings. **What to add** then shows the doses for that pool.
- **Chemicals added**, **Parts used**, the **Checklist** and **Photos** record what you did.
- **Note to the homeowner** goes on the customer's report; the **Office note** is only for the office.
- **Open work orders** on that pool are shown on the visit.
- **Finish visit** saves it (offline it says "sends when you're back online").
- **New report** adds a pool that isn't on your route as an extra visit (**Find a pool**). It needs a connection.

## My Route (/schedule)

The schedule board for any day: your route and the other techs'. Use the arrows or **Today** to change the day.

- With Agent Plus, you can rearrange your own route (drag stops, or **Optimize order** for the shortest drive) and use **Skip stop** / **Unskip stop** on your own stops.
- Without Agent Plus, ask the office to skip, move or reassign a stop.

## Reports (/reports)

Your finished visits (with Agent Plus, everyone's). Open one to see the water test, chemicals, parts, photos and notes, and to correct it (**Save**).

## Work orders (/work-orders)

Repairs and one-off jobs assigned to you (with Agent Plus, all of them; **Mine** shows only yours). Open one to read the details, **Add note**, and mark it **Done** when it's finished.

## Your own settings

Click your name at the bottom of the menu:

- **Profile** (name, email, photo), **Password**, **Two-factor**, **Appearance** (light or dark, **Big view** for larger text) and **Emails**.
- **Notifications** (/notifications): what you're told about, and pop-ups or phone alerts.
- The bottom of the menu also shows whether you're online, and **Share location** (this phone shares where you are during your route, so the office map is right).
