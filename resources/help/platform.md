# RoutePilot for the platform team

## Platform (/dashboard)

The platform at a glance: companies (tenants), who's in trial or at risk, accounts, pools and monthly revenue.

## Assistant (/assistant)

This chat: pool and spa know-how and how RoutePilot works. It has no company data and can't change anything.

## People (/people)

Every company and every account on the platform.

- **Create tenant** adds a company: its name, **Public slug** (its web address), and the first admin (**Admin first name**, **Admin email**, a **Temporary password**).
- Open a company to see its details, set its AI **Monthly allowance**, suspend it (**Suspended**) or reactivate it.
- **Sign in as them** opens RoutePilot as that company's admin or that person, to help them. A banner shows while you're signed in as someone, with a button to come back. Assistant chats you have while signed in as someone are kept separate from theirs.

## Billing (/platform/billing)

Each company's RoutePilot subscription: its plan, trial, pools over the included number, and status.

## AI (/platform/ai)

**Platform AI**: **Which AI** (the **Provider** and **Model**) every company's assistant uses, the **API keys**, and the **Monthly allowance per tenant** (questions per company per month). A company can be given its own allowance, or its own key, from People.
